# Support

## Project status

This repository is a pre-release open-source candidate. Community support channels
activate after Phase 16 publication.

## Bug reports

After the repository is published, use GitHub Issues for reproducible bugs.
Include environment details and sanitized logs. Do not include secrets or customer data.

## Questions and ideas

After GitHub Discussions is enabled, use Discussions for general questions.

Until then, non-security questions may be directed to https://cited.cc/contact.

## Security issues

Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not report vulnerabilities in public issues.

## Self-hosting support

Self-hosting documentation is available under [docs/](docs/README.md):

- [Quickstart](docs/getting-started/quickstart.md)
- [Troubleshooting](docs/operations/troubleshooting.md)
- [Configuration reference](docs/reference/environment-variables.md)

Community self-hosting support is best-effort unless a separate commercial agreement
states otherwise.

## Cited Cloud

Hosted Cited at cited.cc is a separate product with its own support terms.
Open-source repository support does not imply Cited Cloud service-level commitments.
Cloud functionality and plans may change.

## Response times

No response time guarantees are implied for community support or pre-release candidates.

## Commercial support

Commercial support may be offered separately in the future. No pricing is published here.
