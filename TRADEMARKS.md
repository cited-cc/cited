the software license in LICENSE.

services.

This document does not claim that any mark is registered unless registration

endorsement.

## Permitted referential use

Fair referential use is permitted when it is truthful and not misleading.
Examples include:

- "Based on Cited"
- "Compatible with Cited"
- "A fork of the Cited open-source project"

Do not use Cited branding as your own product name unless you have written
permission.

## Forks and modified deployments

Public forks and unofficial deployments should use distinct branding unless
written permission is granted.

If you modify Cited and make it available to others, clearly disclose that your
deployment is unofficial and not operated by the Cited project or Cited Cloud.

Do not imply that a fork, mirror, or modified deployment is the official Cited
service at cited.cc unless that is true.

Until the public repository is published, contact the project through
https://cited.cc/contact for trademark questions.
